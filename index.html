Method

  1. Start with the one-of-a-kind results: ammonia = NH₄Cl + NaOH, brown = AgNO₃ + NaOH, cream = AgNO₃ + KBr.
  2. AgNO₃ reacts with all six others. An unknown with any “No change” cannot be AgNO₃.
  3. A white precipitate needs Ag⁺ or Ba²⁺ in one of the pair.
  4. A little white precipitate forming slowly is sparingly soluble Ag₂SO₄.
  5. Before committing, test your full assignment against every cell, including the “No change” ones.
